# Style Menus

Use these option pools to recommend 2-3 choices tailored to the user's story. Do not dump the full list to the user.

## Director Style Options

- Akira Kurosawa, `Seven Samurai` / `Ikiru`: moral pressure, weather, social groups, strong blocking, human dignity.
- Yasujirō Ozu, `Tokyo Story`: restrained family emotion, quiet interiors, still compositions, everyday sorrow.
- Hirokazu Kore-eda, `Shoplifters` / `Nobody Knows`: poverty, found family, understated realism, moral ambiguity.
- Wong Kar-wai, `In the Mood for Love`: memory, longing, color, slow motion, intimate urban melancholy.
- Ang Lee, `Eat Drink Man Woman` / `Life of Pi`: family bonds, warmth, food, emotional restraint, lyrical realism.
- Steven Spielberg, `Schindler's List` / `E.T.`: accessible emotional clarity, child perspective, strong visual storytelling.
- Christopher Nolan, `Dunkirk` / `Interstellar`: tension, time structure, large-scale suspense, precise cross-cutting.
- Wes Anderson, `The Grand Budapest Hotel`: symmetrical framing, whimsical melancholy, theatrical production design.
- Hayao Miyazaki, `Spirited Away` / `My Neighbor Totoro`: wonder, children, compassion, hand-crafted animated worlds.
- Satoshi Kon, `Tokyo Godfathers`: urban coincidence, social outsiders, kinetic animated realism.
- Guillermo del Toro, `Pan's Labyrinth`: fairy-tale darkness, tactile worlds, empathy for vulnerable characters.
- Denis Villeneuve, `Arrival` / `Blade Runner 2049`: quiet awe, atmospheric scale, controlled visual tension.
- Chloé Zhao, `Nomadland`: natural light, nonprofessional realism, poetic observation.
- Jia Zhangke, `Still Life` / `A Touch of Sin`: social realism, ordinary people, changing towns, observational distance.
- Zhang Yimou, `Raise the Red Lantern` / `Not One Less`: strong color, social ritual, rural or town morality, clear emotional staging.

## Aspect Ratio Options

- `16:9`: standard video, YouTube, storyboard sheets, animation previews, most general use.
- `2.39:1`: cinematic widescreen, stronger loneliness, town streets, moral drama, theatrical film feeling.
- `1.85:1`: classic cinema balance for character drama and interiors.
- `4:3`: nostalgic, intimate, documentary or old-TV feeling.
- `9:16`: mobile vertical short, TikTok/Reels/Shorts, single-character emotional immediacy.
- `1:1`: social feed, compact visual beats, posters or mood thumbnails.

For generated 3x3 storyboard sheets, use the selected target ratio for both the overall sheet and each panel. If the user gives no ratio preference, default to `16:9`.

## Video Nature Options

- 电影: cinematic pacing, complete emotional arc, strongest production value.
- 微电影: short but complete story, restrained dialogue, emotional twist ending.
- 动画短片: stylized performance, clear silhouettes, family-friendly or poetic tone.
- 公益广告: compact moral message, strong empathy, memorable final image.
- 品牌宣传片: positive association, product/place warmth, polished visuals.
- 纪录片: observational realism, natural light, minimal staging, social texture.
- 手持手机拍摄: raw immediacy, low-budget realism, subjective proximity.
- 短视频剧情片: quick hook, clear beats, vertical or 16:9 adaptation, strong emotional payoff.
- 音乐视频: mood-led montage, lyrical transitions, less dialogue reliance.

## Recommendation Heuristics

- Child poverty, ordinary kindness, and moral ambiguity: prefer Kore-eda, Ozu, Ang Lee, Jia Zhangke, Zhang Yimou.
- Food, warmth, family, and community: prefer Ang Lee, Miyazaki, Spielberg, Kore-eda.
- Suspense, theft, accusation, and crowd pressure: include Kurosawa or Spielberg when stronger drama helps.
- Gentle healing or fairy-tale warmth: include Miyazaki, Pixar-like animation, claymation, or warm realism.
- Social realism or ethical discomfort: include Kore-eda, Jia Zhangke, black-and-white documentary, or handheld realism.
- Public-service message: include microfilm or public-service ad as video nature.
